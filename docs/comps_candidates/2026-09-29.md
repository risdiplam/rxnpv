**Three things worth knowing:**
- **Fabhalta is mislabelled.** $505M with +291% is Novartis's **2025** figure. The app files it under 2024, so its launch curve currently looks a year faster than it really was.
- **Eliquis and Biktarvy were wrong even for 2024.** BMS reported Eliquis at $13,333M for 2024, and Gilead reported Biktarvy at $13,423M, not $12.9B. I don't know where 12.9 came from. Either way, 2025 replaces it.
- **Trikafta has stopped growing.** Sales rose less than 1% in 2025 while Vertex's newer drug Alyftrek did $838M. The status line "still growing" is no longer true. Also, the entry's name says "Vanzatri", but Vertex's brand name for vanzacaftor is **Alyftrek**. It's worth fixing the name while you're there.
